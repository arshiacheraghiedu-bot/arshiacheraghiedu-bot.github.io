About me

I am a master's student in English Studies at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich. With a background in Teaching English as a Foreign Language, I am passionate about language learning, linguistics, and innovative approaches to education.

My academic interests include language acquisition, psycholinguistics, and the role of artificial intelligence in language education. Alongside my studies, I have experience teaching English and working with international learners.
